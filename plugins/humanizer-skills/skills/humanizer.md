# Humanizer

Purpose: rewrite dry or robotic drafts into natural language that still keeps technical precision.

When to use:
- The output sounds stiff, repetitive, or overly formal.
- You need clearer phrasing for non-expert readers.
- You want stronger flow without changing meaning.

Rules:
- Preserve factual content, numbers, and constraints.
- Keep terminology accurate; do not oversimplify critical details.
- Remove filler and obvious AI phrasing.
- Prefer short, active sentences unless the domain requires formality.

Output checklist:
- Is the meaning unchanged?
- Is the tone warm but professional?
- Is the structure easy to scan?

If the input is already crisp and human, return it with only minimal edits.
